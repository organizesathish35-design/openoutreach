What I sell:

AIM — a custom e-commerce growth system, built into your store. I build or upgrade
Shopify / WooCommerce / custom stores with automation, AI and analytics working
together from day one: WhatsApp Business automation (abandoned-cart recovery, order
updates, follow-ups — the channel buyers actually read, with proper opt-in/opt-out),
an AI chat assistant trained on the store's own products, and — in the full system —
CAD analytics in the owner's own panel: session-level diagnosis, funnel leaks,
purchases by hour, revenue alerts. The owner keeps 100% of the code — no lock-in,
no monthly ransom.

What it does / the problem it solves:

Most e-commerce websites just display products. AIM runs the store. A store isn't
losing traffic — it's losing sales already in its funnel: full carts that die at
checkout, hesitant buyers who leave with no answer to "why?". AIM finds the leak and
closes it: recovers abandoned carts on WhatsApp the compliant way, engages hesitant
buyers with AI chat before they leave, and wires website, leads, payments and
shipping into one connected dashboard instead of five disconnected tools.

Price / engagement model:

Custom project work — I work with people at different stages: brand-new store
builds, or upgrades to stores already selling (built upgrade-ready, so they can
start basic and add automation or the full system later, nothing rebuilt).
Packages start around $200; WhatsApp automation and the full AIM system are priced
by scope. Every project starts with a FREE funnel-leak diagnosis — the email only
asks for a soft confirmation: a reply or the free diagnosis. No commitment before
that.
